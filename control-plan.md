# Control plan — educational template

| Item | Operational definition | Owner role | Review | Evidence / response |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Changeover time | Last prior-format good unit to first next-format accepted unit | Shift lead | Every changeover | Timestamp record; review deviation with event notes |
| First-pass acceptance | First inspection passes without rework | Quality role | Every restart | Approved inspection record; follow local nonconformance process |
| Parts readiness | Correct approved parts staged before scheduled change | Setup coordinator | Before change | Readiness record; escalate missing items |
| Data consistency | Format, staffing and interruption fields complete | Process analyst | Weekly | Flag missing values; do not silently impute |

Acceptance criteria and escalation thresholds must be agreed for the actual process. No limits are invented here. Action closure requires evidence and effectiveness review, not only a checked box.
